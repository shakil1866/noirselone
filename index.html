About First Look

A professional unisex hair and beauty salon

Welcome to First Look, a professional unisex hair and beauty salon located on VIP Road, Six Mile, Guwahati.

We bring together professional hair care, beauty, skincare, makeup and grooming services in one comfortable salon environment. Whether you want a fresh haircut, a new hairstyle, professional makeup, skincare treatment or complete grooming, our goal is to help you look and feel your best.

At First Look, we focus on personalized service and creating a style that suits your personality, preferences and occasion.
