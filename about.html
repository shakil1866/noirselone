About First Look

Welcome to **First Look**, a professional unisex hair and beauty salon located on VIP Road in Six Mile, Guwahati.

We provide a complete range of professional hair, beauty and grooming services designed for both men and women. From stylish haircuts and hair treatments to skincare, makeup, nail care and men's grooming, we bring a variety of beauty services together under one roof.

Our focus is on providing a comfortable salon experience while helping every client find a style and look that suits their personality, preferences and occasion.

Whether you are looking for a fresh haircut, a new hairstyle, a relaxing beauty treatment, professional makeup or complete grooming, First Look is ready to help you look and feel your best.
